Proofpay

Pay for what the camera proved, at the friction your history earned.

A trust-scored, pay-on-verify checkout on PayPal, for camera-tracked stores and AI buying agents.

At a glance

Hold the basket. Capture only what was verified.

1 · Vision

Each item gets a probability it was taken.

Billable only at p ≥ 0.90 or staff-confirmed.

2 · Trust engine

Payer history picks Express, Standard or Strict.

By expected cost, explained line by line.

3 · PayPal

Orders v2 authorize, Payments v2 capture / void / refund.

Webhooks feed the trust log.

4 · AI

Gemini explains uncertain lines and triages “Not mine” claims.

Staff decide; agreement is logged.

Stack

FastAPI · SQLite · Next.js static export · AG Grid · Docker on Render

In this recording

PayPal runs on an offline stand-in with the same amount rules; AI on its template fallback. The live sandbox run is next.

How trust works

Trust changes friction. Never the charge.

Reputation

Beta(α, β) per PayPal payer.

Verified captures add α; items found at exit, rejected disputes and chargebacks add β.

Caution

Decisions use the 10th percentile, not the mean.

90-day decay; new payers start uncertain.

Anti-gaming

Weekly cap on positive evidence.

Unusually large baskets can’t use Express.

Policy

Cheapest expected cost: unchecked items + hidden items + wrong charges + staff and wait time.

Strict hold

Basket + 15% authorized up front.

PayPal caps over-capture at 115% or USD 75.

Agents

Limit ₹500 → ₹20,000 as score rises.

Up to 3× asks a human; above is denied.
